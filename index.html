    Young Stewards · Learn outdoors

    Care for a place you love

    Free activities for kids, families, classes, and youth groups. Pick a public trail or a place you have permission to visit. Go with a trusted adult and follow the site’s rules.

    • Habitat detectives. Look for three things an animal needs: food, water, and shelter. Sketch what you find and explain how one change might help or hurt that habitat.
    • Follow the rain. From a trail, look for clues about where rainwater goes. Compare bare ground with a patch of leaves or plants. Which one might slow runoff?
    • Leave it better. With an adult, join an approved cleanup or trail workday. Record what your group did and discuss what would prevent the same problem next time.

    Preservation protects places and natural features. Conservation includes caring for them over time. Keep names, photos, and exact wildlife locations out of anything shared publicly.

    Explore the Wild Heritage Legacy Circle

    Conservation where it can do the most good

    The Legacy Circle is designed to support conservation projects across the United States. Projects could restore wildlife habitat, protect waterways, remove invasive species, or care for land already set aside for conservation.

    How projects would be prioritized

    1. Urgency. What could be lost or damaged if the work waits?
    2. Ecological benefit. How much would the work help wildlife, water, or connected habitat?
    3. Readiness. Is there a qualified partner with permission, a realistic budget, and a plan to do the work?
    4. Lasting impact. Who will care for the site afterward, and how will we know the project worked?

    For each approved project, we plan to publish why it was selected, who is responsible, and how the work and spending will be tracked.

      Pick a map mission

      Go with an adult. Tap the map where you made a discovery and choose a matching pin type.

      • 🐾 Track detective · Spot a footprint or other sign from the trail.
      • 💧 Follow the rain · Find where water might flow after a storm.
      • 🌿 Leaf lookout · Compare two leaves without picking them.
      • 🪶 Habitat hideout · Look for a place an animal could shelter.

      Discovery pins stay on this device unless you export them. Leave exact wildlife locations out of anything you share.

      Plant and fungus field notes

      Mark your find with a private pin. In the notes, record the date, habitat, what it was growing on, and the features you noticed. Revisit the same spot across seasons to see what changes.

      These are observation pins. The app does not identify species or determine edibility.

      Hourly wind & weather

      Tap the map to select a point, then load the forecast. Wind direction shows where the wind comes from. Loading sends the selected coordinates rounded to three decimals to the National Weather Service.
